# Known issues and limitations

- No backend, accounts, identity checks, cloud sync, delivery status, push notifications, or live presence. Chat requests, groups, and direct threads are device-only previews.
- The conversation content and request cards are sample preview content, not live users or fabricated live activity. Group membership, shared media views, reporting/blocking, and real contact discovery are not connected.
- Chat previews, including supported small attachments, are browser-local and may be visible to anyone using the same browser profile. Do not enter sensitive information.
- Voice and video calls are intentionally excluded from Chats. Campus voice remains a separate future lecture feature in the supplied product brief.
- Profile fields and theme choice are also saved in browser local storage only. They are not a remote account or published profile.
- Most product areas remain unimplemented. Navigation identifies the intended areas; it does not imply those systems exist.
- Browser preview was reviewed at a 714px viewport. Android and iPhone devices, a wide desktop viewport, and a formal accessibility audit have not been independently verified.
