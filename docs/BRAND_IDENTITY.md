# Night Culture brand identity

## Promise and voice

**Your people. Your world.** Warm, confident, culturally fluent, and clear. Supporting line: **Chat privately. Share publicly. Meet in Campus Life.** Use plain, active interface language and make audience/privacy consequences explicit.

## Visual language

Warm paper, deep green-black, and a sharp citron signal form the current interface palette, with burnt coral used sparingly. This direction supersedes the original purple-first interface palette at the owner's request. Use semantic tokens and contrast checks described in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. The wordmark uses Syne semibold/bold with a distinct `GHETTO APP` lockup.

## Mark direction

Explore signal-link, open-circuit, and portal marks. Current prototype uses an original lowercase portal-style `g` paired with a dotted campus/map texture as a provisional visual cue. It is not trademark-reviewed and should not be represented as a final registered identity.

Do not use telephone-in-speech-bubble marks, distressed graffiti typography, generic esports styling, or another messaging brand's colors/signatures.
