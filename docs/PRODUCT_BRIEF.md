# Product brief

## Product

GHETTO APP is a social communication and interactive community platform for Nigerian and African digital culture. Its promise is **Your people. Your world.** People can chat privately, share publicly through Street Views, and meet in Campus Life.

## Audience and job

Students, alumni, and people connected by universities, communities, crews, and regions need a welcoming place to communicate and take part in campus culture. Private messaging should feel personal; public discovery should be explicit; interactive spaces should feel lively and safe.

## Product identity

Night Culture is confident, warm, youthful, and technically polished. It uses rounded geometry and a neutral dark/light foundation, violet for primary actions, and orange sparingly for live or expressive emphasis. It must not imitate WhatsApp or generic social media.

## Principles

- Real functionality and honest status over decorative controls.
- Privacy by default, with server-enforced access rules.
- Mobile usability, accessible interaction, and clear failure states.
- Avoid fabricated activity and unsupported security claims.
- Build incrementally and keep free-tier costs visible.

## Primary product areas

1. Chats, contacts, requests, and groups.
2. Street Views public social discovery.
3. Explore / The Yard, Communities, and Crews.
4. Campus Life virtual spaces and live classes.
5. Profiles, notifications, settings, quests, and safety tools.
